Privacy Policy

Mystery Crate Mayhem · last updated October 2026

The short version

There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The game doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your player profiles (name, colors, pattern), quest progress, unlocked patterns, keybinds and sound setting are saved in your device's local browser storage. They never leave your device unless you play online (see below). You can delete them at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its site data.

Online play

When you host or join an online game, the app uses the free public PeerJS matchmaking service (0.peerjs.com) to connect you with the other players using a room code. After that, game data goes directly between the players' devices. If a direct connection isn't possible, it may be relayed through PeerJS's relay (TURN) servers. During an online game, the other players and these services can see your network (IP) address. Other players also see your chosen player name, colors and in-game actions. Nothing is stored after the game ends.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Player names typed in the lobby are only shown to the other players in that match.
