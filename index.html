Who is the boss?

Bots

6

Everyone lands with 100 health, 0 shield and a pickaxe. Carry up to 5 items. Open crates with your interact key or smash them. Chop trees for wood, press F for build mode, pick wall, floor or ramp with the mouse wheel or 1–3, and click to place (10 wood each). Ramps let you climb onto floors. Esc pauses. Leave game sits under the minimap.

Hazards

  • Tornado · flings anyone it touches, then swaps everybody's positions when it fizzles out.
  • Meteor shower · red circles mark where rocks land. Move!
  • Supply drop · golden crates with the good stuff land inside the safe zone.
  • The storm · a purple ring closes in. Standing outside it drains your health.
